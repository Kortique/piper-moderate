sprint 2026-05: full relabel + extended training scope (V6c/V8cs80/V11cs80)

After a full manual relabel of 9,150 items (1,469 child / 1,783 teen / 5,898 adult),
all three LGBM models were retrained with an extended training scope that
includes K30 (Tom's external dataset, 6,133 confirmed items) alongside LS and
Grafana. Training set grew 3x (from ~2,500 to ~7,300 items).
